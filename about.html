Our Roots

It Started With Teaching

Long before taking on enterprise client projects, Codfis started with whiteboard sessions and mentoring students in Chennai.

We saw firsthand how many learners were eager to build a career in technology, but felt intimidated by confusing jargon and purely theoretical tutorials.

So we focused on something simpler: sitting down with people, demystifying technology, building practical skills step-by-step, and giving them the confidence to solve real problems independently.
