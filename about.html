Our Roots

It Started With Teaching

Long before taking on client projects, Codfis started with whiteboard sessions, open discussions, and mentoring students in Chennai.

We saw firsthand how many learners were eager to build a career in technology, but felt overwhelmed by confusing jargon and purely theoretical classes.

So we focused on something simpler: sitting down with people, demystifying technology, building practical skills step-by-step, and giving them the confidence to stand on their own feet.

Hands-on Mentorship
Focusing on real understanding over memorizing slides.
Confidence & Growth
Empowering learners to solve problems independently.
Evolution

Then Came Another Question

If we can teach people how to write clean, dependable software…
why aren’t we building those solutions for businesses that need them?

Taking that step was a natural progression. We brought the same care, patience, and attention to detail from the classroom directly into building web applications and digital tools for businesses.
